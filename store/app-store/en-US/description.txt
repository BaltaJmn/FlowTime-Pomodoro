Work for as long as it feels right and your break adapts. FlowTime is a pomodoro timer for study and productivity: three focus modes, no ads and no account.

THREE WAYS TO FOCUS
Pomodoro: fixed blocks of work and rest, so you can start without thinking.
FlowTime: no countdown while you work; when you stop, your break comes from how long you worked. When you are in the zone, nothing cuts you off.
Percentage: your break is the percentage you choose of the time you worked.
Every mode's times are yours to adjust.

KEEPS COUNTING WHEN YOU CLOSE THE APP
The timer runs on the clock, not on the screen. You get an alert at the end of each phase even if the app is closed.

ON THE LOCK SCREEN, WITHOUT OPENING THE APP
Follow your session from the lock screen and, on iPhones that have it, from the Dynamic Island, and pause or stop it right from there. With the home screen widget you start, pause and resume a session without opening the app.

TODAY'S TASKS
Write down what you want to get done today. Whatever you don't finish shows up the next day, so nothing gets lost. Pick a task for each session and, at the break, the app asks whether you finished it.

TAGS
Study, work, reading, languages: tag your sessions and see where your time goes.

STREAK, GOAL AND STATS
Set a daily goal, keep your streak and level up. See your day, week and month: total time, sessions, averages and your best day.

SOUNDS TO FOCUS
Rain, fire, waves, thunder, birds, heat, a coffee shop, meditation, wind, and brown, pink and white noise, generated on your iPhone and still playing with the screen locked. Mix them, save your mixes and set a timer so they fade out on their own.

MADE YOURS
Dark mode and 11 themes. An optional daily reminder, plus backup through your iPhone's iCloud backup and backup to a file. In 14 languages.

FREE, NO ADS
All three modes, the session on the lock screen, the widget, up to 15 pending tasks, 4 tags, all 12 sounds with 3 saved mixes, your streak, your goal, stats for today, the week and the month, and backups. No ads, no account, no trial.

PRO, ONE PAYMENT
