FlowTime Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

English - Español

The short version. FlowTime is a focus timer. Your sessions, tasks, tags, goals and settings are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded to us. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The only data that leaves your phone is what is needed to know whether you have bought FlowTime Pro or left a tip.

Who is responsible

FlowTime ("the app") is published by Baltasar Jiménez ("we", "us"), an individual developer based in Spain, who acts as data controller for the limited processing described below.

Contact for anything in this policy: baltax.75@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in FlowTime is stored locally, in the app's private storage:

  • Your sessions: when each one started and ended, its mode (Pomodoro, FlowTime or Percentage), and the tag and task you chose for it, if any.
  • Your tasks and tags, your daily goal and your streak.
  • Your settings: timer lengths, theme, app icon, sounds and saved sound mixes, reminder time, and whether you have bought FlowTime Pro or left a tip.
  • The session in progress, so that the timer keeps counting when the app is closed.

The home screen widget, the quick settings button on Android and the Live Activity on iPhone read the session in progress from the same storage. Notifications, alerts and the daily reminder are created on your device. The ambient sounds are generated by the app itself; nothing is streamed.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Your phone's own backup

If backup is turned on in your phone, Android includes FlowTime's sessions, tasks, tags and settings in the backup it makes to your Google account, and in a direct transfer to a new phone. The session in progress is left out. That backup belongs to your Google account and is covered by Google's policies. We cannot read it.

On iPhone, if iCloud Backup is turned on, iOS includes FlowTime's data in the backup it makes to your iCloud account, and the same happens with a backup to a computer. That backup belongs to your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's policies. We cannot read it either.

Files you export

FlowTime can save a backup file with your sessions, tasks, tags and settings, and FlowTime Pro can export your sessions as a CSV file. You choose where each file is saved. Once saved, it is outside our control, and if it goes to a cloud folder or another app, that provider's policy applies to it, not this one. Importing a backup file only reads the file you pick.

What leaves your device

Purchases

FlowTime offers FlowTime Pro, a one-time purchase, and optional tips. Payments are processed by the store you installed FlowTime from, Google Play or the App Store; we never see or receive your card details, billing address or any other payment information.

To know whether an installation has bought FlowTime Pro or left a tip, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat assigns each installation an anonymous identifier and stores, on our behalf, that identifier, the purchase history associated with it, and technical information such as the app version, the operating system version and the store country. This identifier is never linked to your name or your email address, and nothing about your sessions, tasks or tags is ever sent to RevenueCat. FlowTime does not use the advertising identifier. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

RevenueCat processes data in the United States. Transfers outside the European Economic Area are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Ratings

FlowTime may show the store's own rating dialog, from Google Play or the App Store. Your rating goes to that store, under its own policy; the app is not told what you rated or whether you rated at all.

Nothing else

FlowTime contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not build profiles, we do not use remarketing, and we do not sell or share personal information with anyone. The app needs an internet connection only for purchases.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Purchase records held by RevenueCat and by the store are kept for as long as those services require in order to honour restores and to meet their own legal and tax obligations.

Children

FlowTime is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability.

Most of these you can exercise yourself: the data is on your phone. Deleting a task, or a session when you edit a day's history, erases it, uninstalling the app erases everything, and the backup file is data portability. For the purchase data held by RevenueCat, including a request to delete it, write to baltax.75@gmail.com with your Google Play or App Store order number, which is the only way we can find your records, precisely because we hold nothing that identifies you.

Legal bases: performance of a contract (delivering the purchase you made) and our legitimate interest in preventing fraudulent restores. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. In Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Your rights under the CCPA / CPRA

If you are a California resident: in the last twelve months we have collected the categories "identifiers" (an anonymous installation identifier) and "commercial information" (your purchase history), for the sole purpose of delivering the purchase you made. We have not sold or shared personal information, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. You have the right to know, to delete, to correct and to non-discrimination for exercising those rights; write to baltax.75@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date at the top, and material changes will also be described in the app's release notes in the store.

FlowTime, política de privacidad

English - Español

En corto. FlowTime es un temporizador de concentración. Tus sesiones, tareas, etiquetas, objetivos y ajustes se guardan solo en tu móvil y no se nos envían nunca. No hay cuenta, ni publicidad, ni analítica, ni seguimiento. Lo único que sale de tu móvil es lo necesario para saber si has comprado FlowTime Pro o has dejado una propina.

Quién es el responsable

FlowTime ("la app") está publicada por Baltasar Jiménez ("nosotros"), desarrollador individual con residencia en España, que actúa como responsable del tratamiento limitado que se describe abajo.

Contacto para cualquier asunto de esta política: baltax.75@gmail.com.

Lo que se queda en tu dispositivo

Todo lo que creas en FlowTime se guarda en local, en el almacenamiento privado de la app:

  • Tus sesiones: cuándo empezó y terminó cada una, su modo (Pomodoro, FlowTime o Porcentaje) y la etiqueta y la tarea que elegiste, si las hay.
  • Tus tareas y etiquetas, tu objetivo diario y tu racha.
  • Tus ajustes: los tiempos del temporizador, el tema, el icono de la app, los sonidos y las mezclas guardadas, la hora del recordatorio y si has comprado FlowTime Pro o dejado una propina.
  • La sesión en curso, para que el temporizador siga contando con la app cerrada.

El widget de la pantalla de inicio, el botón de los ajustes rápidos en Android y la actividad en directo en el iPhone leen la sesión en curso del mismo almacenamiento. Las notificaciones, los avisos y el recordatorio diario se generan en tu dispositivo. Los sonidos ambientales los genera la propia app; no se descarga nada.

Nada de esto se nos envía ni se envía a terceros. Desinstalar la app lo borra.

La copia de seguridad de tu propio móvil

Si tienes activada la copia de seguridad del móvil, Android incluye las sesiones, tareas, etiquetas y ajustes de FlowTime en la copia que hace a tu cuenta de Google, y en el paso directo a un móvil nuevo. La sesión en curso se queda fuera. Esa copia pertenece a tu cuenta de Google y se rige por las políticas de Google. Nosotros no podemos leerla.

En el iPhone, si tienes activada la copia en iCloud, iOS incluye los datos de FlowTime en la copia que hace a tu cuenta de iCloud, y lo mismo pasa con una copia en un ordenador. Esa copia pertenece a tu Cuenta de Apple y se rige por las políticas de Apple. Tampoco podemos leerla.

Los ficheros que exportas

FlowTime puede guardar un fichero de copia de seguridad con tus sesiones, tareas, etiquetas y ajustes, y FlowTime Pro puede exportar tus sesiones a un fichero CSV. Tú eliges dónde se guarda cada fichero. Una vez guardado queda fuera de nuestro control, y si va a una carpeta en la nube o a otra app, se le aplica la política de ese proveedor, no esta. Importar una copia solo lee el fichero que eliges.

Lo que sí sale de tu dispositivo

Compras

FlowTime ofrece FlowTime Pro, de pago único, y propinas opcionales. El pago lo procesa la tienda desde la que instalaste FlowTime, Google Play o la App Store; nosotros no vemos ni recibimos los datos de tu tarjeta, tu dirección de facturación ni ningún otro dato de pago.

Para saber si una instalación ha comprado FlowTime Pro o ha dejado una propina, la app usa RevenueCat, Inc. como encargado del tratamiento. RevenueCat asigna a cada instalación un identificador anónimo y guarda, por cuenta nuestra, ese identificador, el historial de compras asociado y datos técnicos como la versión de la app, la versión del sistema operativo y el país de la tienda. Ese identificador nunca se vincula a tu nombre ni a tu correo, y nada de tus sesiones, tareas o etiquetas se envía nunca a RevenueCat. FlowTime no usa el identificador de publicidad. Consulta la política de privacidad de RevenueCat.

RevenueCat trata los datos en Estados Unidos. Las transferencias fuera del Espacio Económico Europeo están amparadas por las Cláusulas Contractuales Tipo de la Comisión Europea.

Valoraciones

FlowTime puede mostrar el diálogo de valoración de la propia tienda, Google Play o la App Store. Tu valoración va a esa tienda y se rige por su política; la app no sabe qué nota pusiste ni si valoraste.

Nada más

FlowTime no incluye ningún SDK de publicidad, de analítica ni de informes de fallos. No elaboramos perfiles, no usamos remarketing y no vendemos ni compartimos información personal con nadie. La app necesita conexión a internet solo para las compras.
