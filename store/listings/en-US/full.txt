Work for as long as it feels right and your break adapts. FlowTime is a pomodoro timer for study and productivity: three focus modes and no ads. No account needed.

THREE WAYS TO FOCUS
Pomodoro: fixed blocks of work and rest, so you can start without thinking.
FlowTime: no countdown while you work; when you stop, your break comes from how long you worked. When you are in the zone, nothing cuts you off.
Percentage: your break is the percentage you choose of the time you worked.
Every mode's times are yours to adjust.

STUDY WITH YOUR FRIENDS
Coop is a private room for up to 8 people with one timer for everyone: anyone can start, pause or skip the break. Join with a code or a link, or invite your friends, and get a notification when someone invites you to a room or sends you a friend request. No chat and no public rooms, and you can block or report anyone. Coop asks you to sign in with Google; the rest of the app still needs no account.

KEEPS COUNTING WHEN YOU CLOSE THE APP
The timer runs on the clock, not on the screen. A notification shows the timer with buttons to pause and stop, and you get an alert at the end of each phase even if the app is closed.

WITHOUT OPENING THE APP
A home screen widget and a quick settings tile: start and stop a session right from there.

TODAY'S TASKS
Write down what you want to get done today. Whatever you don't finish shows up the next day, so nothing gets lost. Pick a task for each session and, at the break, the app asks whether you finished it.

TAGS
Study, work, reading, languages: tag your sessions and see where your time goes.

STREAK, GOAL AND STATS
Set a daily goal, keep your streak and level up. See your day, week and month: total time, sessions, averages and your best day.

SOUNDS TO FOCUS
Rain, fire, waves, thunder, birds, heat, a coffee shop, meditation, wind, and brown, pink and white noise, generated on your phone. Mix them, save your mixes and set a timer so they fade out on their own.

MADE YOURS
Dark mode, your wallpaper colors and 11 themes. An optional daily reminder, plus automatic backup and backup to a file. In English, Spanish, German, Italian, Russian and Hindi.

FREE, NO ADS
All three modes, the notification, the widget, the quick settings tile, up to 15 pending tasks, 4 tags, all 12 sounds with 3 saved mixes, your streak, your goal, stats for today, the week and the month, backups and Coop. No ads, no trial, and no account needed.

PRO, YOUR WAY
Advanced stats (the whole year, comparison with the previous period, by hour, by mode, by tag and the tasks you spent the most time on) and CSV export; unlimited tags, pending tasks and mixes; 6 new sounds, 6 new themes and 6 new icons; and automatic Do Not Disturb while you work. One payment for life, or monthly if you prefer.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
Everything stays on your phone, with no tracking. An account is optional and only for Coop.
